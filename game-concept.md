# Game Concept: *STACKRIFT*
*A Hybrid-Casual Puzzle Platformer*

---

## Elevator Pitch

**STACKRIFT** is a one-thumb hybrid-casual puzzle game where players stack, balance, and launch colored blocks across crumbling platforms to reach the exit portal — before the rift swallows everything. Simple to pick up in 10 seconds. Impossible to master. Endlessly replayable through a procedurally-generated level system layered with a deep progression meta.

---

## Core Mechanic

Players control a single character standing on a platform. Each round, colored blocks fall from above. The player:

1. **Taps to catch** a falling block and add it to their stack.
2. **Swipes to launch** the stack — sending blocks as a projectile across a gap to build a bridge, trigger a switch, or knock out an obstacle.
3. **Times their jump** across the newly formed bridge to the exit portal before the platform crumbles.

The challenge scales through:
- Stack height (more blocks = more unstable, but more powerful launches)
- Block color combinations (matching colors = bonus effects — freeze, bounce, explode)
- Platform types (crumbling, moving, electrified, flipping)
- Rift speed (a creeping destruction zone that accelerates as levels progress)

**Session length:** 45–90 seconds per level. Designed for one-handed, portrait-mode play.

---

## Why This Mechanic Works

The core loop hits the three pillars of proven hyper-casual hits:

- **Immediate comprehension:** No tutorial text needed. First action teaches the mechanic.
- **Satisfying feedback loop:** Block stacking is tactile and visually rewarding; launches produce a satisfying arc with particle effects and screen shake.
- **"One more try" hook:** Near-miss failures (stack fell at the last second, block combo just didn't click) trigger an immediate re-attempt reflex.

The mechanic is meaningfully distinct from existing titles. It borrows the tactile feel of *Helix Jump* and the launch/physics satisfaction of *Angry Birds*, but combines them into a forward-momentum puzzle structure not currently occupied by a top-10 title.

---

## Meta-Game Layer (Hybrid-Casual Depth)

Beyond the core loop, STACKRIFT includes three retention-driving meta systems:

### 1. The Rift Codex (Progression System)
Players earn "Rift Shards" by completing levels and achieving combo bonuses. Shards unlock permanent upgrades in a branching tech tree:
- **Platform Upgrades:** Extended crumble timers, platform freezing tools
- **Block Upgrades:** Enhanced color combo effects, wider catch radius
- **Character Cosmetics:** Skins, trails, portal animations (no pay-to-win)

This tree creates a 60–90 day core progression arc before a prestige/season reset.

### 2. World Zones (Episodic Content)
The game is organized into 6 launch Zones, each with 30 levels and a distinct visual biome:
- Zone 1: Ancient Ruins (tutorial pacing)
- Zone 2: Neon City (introduces moving platforms)
- Zone 3: Ice Caverns (introduces physics modifiers)
- Zone 4: Volcanic Rift (introduces time pressure)
- Zone 5: Sky Temples (introduces wind mechanics)
- Zone 6: The Core (boss-level finale)

Post-launch, zones are added via LiveOps content drops every 6–8 weeks.

### 3. Daily Challenges & Leaderboards
A daily "Rift Run" mode presents a fixed level seed — identical for all players on that day. Players compete for top-100 placement on a global leaderboard. Top performers receive cosmetic rewards. This system drives D7 and D30 retention through social competition without requiring guild infrastructure.

## Visual & Audio Identity

**Art Style:** Bold, saturated flat-3D aesthetic. Think *Monument Valley* meets *Fall Guys* — geometric clarity with expressive color palettes that shift across biomes. Accessible on mid-range devices (targets 60fps on devices ≥ 2019).

**Character Design:** A small, expressive humanoid figure with large eyes and minimal features — maximally readable at small screen sizes, easily cosmeticized.

**Audio:** Punchy, satisfying SFX on every block catch and launch. Adaptive music that escalates with rift proximity. Clean, non-fatiguing loop design for long sessions.

---

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Ages 18–34, casual mobile gamers, both genders. Plays during commutes, breaks, and pre-sleep sessions. Familiar with the genre; attracted by novelty and satisfying feedback.

**Secondary:** Ages 13–17 and 35–44 who gravitate toward puzzle games with replayability. Social-competitive engagement via leaderboards and shareable clip moments.

**Geographic focus (launch):** US, UK, Germany, Brazil, Southeast Asia — high install volume combined with meaningful IAP conversion rates.
