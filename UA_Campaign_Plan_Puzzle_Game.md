# User Acquisition Campaign Plan: Flappy Bird-Style Puzzle Game

## Executive Summary

This document outlines a comprehensive User Acquisition (UA) strategy for launching a casual puzzle game similar to Flappy Bird. The campaign focuses on building an engaged user base through multi-channel marketing, community engagement, and data-driven optimization.

---

## 1. Campaign Objectives

### Primary Goals
- **Download Target**: 100,000+ downloads in first 30 days
- **Installation Rate**: Target 5-10% conversion from ad impressions
- **Cost per Install (CPI)**: $0.50 - $1.50 depending on channel
- **Day 1 Retention (D1)**: 40%+
- **Day 7 Retention (D7)**: 25%+
- **Day 30 Retention (D30)**: 15%+

### Secondary Goals
- Build strong organic growth momentum
- Establish social media presence and community
- Generate user reviews and ratings (target 4.5+ stars)
- Create viral growth loops and referral mechanisms

---

## 2. Target Audience Analysis

### Primary Demographics
- **Age**: 13-45 years old
- **Gender**: All genders (emphasis on casual gamers)
- **Location**: English-speaking markets (US, UK, Canada, Australia) initially

### Player Segments
1. **Casual Gamers** (40%) - Play 15-30 min daily, prefer simple mechanics
2. **Competitive Players** (30%) - Driven by leaderboards and achievements
3. **Social Gamers** (20%) - Enjoy sharing and competing with friends
4. **Retro Enthusiasts** (10%) - Nostalgic for classic arcade gameplay

### Platform Preference
- **Mobile (iOS/Android)**: 85%
- **Web**: 15%

---

## 3. Pre-Launch Phase (Weeks 1-4)

### 3.1 Soft Launch Strategy
- Launch in small markets (Canada, Australia, New Zealand)
- Monitor key metrics: D1, D7, CPI, engagement rates
- Gather user feedback and identify issues
- Duration: 2-3 weeks

### 3.2 Community Building
- **Discord Server**: Create community hub for beta testers
- **Reddit**: Post in r/mobilegaming, r/puzzlegames for early feedback
- **TikTok**: Start building presence with game teaser content
- **Instagram**: Share short gameplay clips and behind-the-scenes content
- Goal: 5,000+ community members before launch

### 3.3 Influencer Outreach
- Identify micro-influencers (10K-100K followers) in gaming niche
- Send early access codes and media kit
- Target at least 20 influencers for launch day content
- Estimated reach: 5-10M impressions

### 3.4 ASO Optimization
- **App Title**: Include primary keywords (e.g., "Flappy Puzzle - Addictive Game")
- **Description**: Highlight core gameplay, simplicity, and challenge
- **Keywords**: puzzle, casual, arcade, flappy, brain teaser, mobile game
- **Screenshots**: Show gameplay progression, difficulty levels, achievements
- **Thumbnail**: Eye-catching, contrasts well, includes game character
- **Category**: Casual (Games)

---

## 4. Launch Phase (Week 5-8)

### 4.1 Paid Advertising Strategy

#### 4.1.1 Mobile App Install Ads
**Facebook & Instagram**
- Budget: $5,000-10,000 week 1
- Target: Lookalike audiences of casual gamers
- Creative: 3-4 different 15-30 second video ads showing gameplay
- Bidding: CPC or CPM, optimize for app installs
- Expected CPI: $0.80-1.20

**Google UAC (Universal App Campaigns)**
- Budget: $5,000-10,000 week 1
- Let Google optimize placements across Google Play, YouTube, Google Search
- Upload 10+ video creatives (various lengths)
- Expected CPI: $0.60-1.00

**TikTok**
- Budget: $3,000-5,000 week 1
- Target: Users interested in gaming, puzzle, entertainment
- Creative: Authentic, entertaining short-form videos
- Expected CPI: $0.50-0.80

**Snapchat**
- Budget: $2,000-3,000 week 1
- Target: Younger demographic (13-25)
- Creative: Interactive snap ads with playable previews
- Expected CPI: $0.70-1.10

#### 4.1.2 Streaming Platform Ads
- **YouTube**: Pre-roll ads on gaming channels (budget: $2,000-3,000)
- **Twitch**: Sponsorships with casual gaming streamers (budget: $1,500-2,000)

#### 4.1.3 Budget Allocation (Week 1)
- Facebook/Instagram: 35%
- Google UAC: 30%
- TikTok: 20%
- Snapchat: 10%
- YouTube/Twitch: 5%
- **Total Week 1**: $17,500-23,000

### 4.2 Organic Strategy
- **App Store Featuring**: Submit to Apple/Google for featured placement
- **Press Release**: Distribute via PRWeb, GlobeNewswire
- **Gaming Media**: Pitch to Pocket Gamer, TouchArcade, AppAdvice
- **Reddit AMAs**: Host "Ask Me Anything" in relevant subreddits
- **Email Marketing**: Launch subscriber list and send announcement

### 4.3 Launch Day Activities
- Simultaneous release across iOS and Android
- Launch day social media blitz (all platforms)
- Influencer content goes live (coordinated)
- Push notifications to beta testers encouraging reviews
- Live streaming launch event (YouTube/Twitch)

---

## 5. Growth Phase (Week 9-16)

### 5.1 Performance-Based Optimization
- **A/B Test Creatives**: Test 5+ new video ads weekly
- **Audience Refinement**: Exclude non-converting segments
- **Bid Strategy**: Adjust based on retention metrics (prioritize D7+ retainers)
- **Channel Optimization**: Scale profitable channels, pause underperformers

### 5.2 Retention-Driven Features
- Implement daily login rewards
- Introduce weekly challenges with unique rewards
- Add leaderboards and achievement badges
- Launch referral program (reward both referrer and friend)
- Push notifications: Optimal timing for re-engagement

### 5.3 Community Engagement
- **Weekly Challenges**: Design themed puzzles (seasonal, event-based)
- **Community Contests**: Host speedrun competitions, fan art contests
- **Discord Events**: Weekly streams, Q&A sessions, community tournaments
- **User-Generated Content**: Showcase fan videos, speedruns, creations

### 5.4 Influencer Partnerships
- Identify top-performing launch influencers
- Negotiate ongoing sponsorship deals (bi-weekly content)
- Create exclusive in-game cosmetics for influencer audiences
- Launch ambassador program (revenue share model)

### 5.5 Paid Advertising Continuation
- **Monthly Budget**: $20,000-30,000 (adjust based on D7 retention)
- Focus on high-performing channels (typically Facebook/Instagram + TikTok)
- Reduce spend on underperforming channels
- Test new platforms based on target demographic insights

---

## 6. Post-Launch Phase (Week 17+)

### 6.1 Content Updates
- **Monthly**: New levels, themes, cosmetics
- **Quarterly**: Major features (multiplayer, seasons, new game modes)
- **Regular Balance**: Adjust difficulty curves based on drop-off data

### 6.2 Monetization Optimization
- Analyze revenue per user (ARPU)
- Test ad placements and rewarded video frequency
- A/B test in-app purchase pricing
- Monitor LTV (Lifetime Value) vs. CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)

### 6.3 Seasonal Campaigns
- **Holiday Events**: Winter, Summer, Halloween themes
- **Special Events**: Limited-time challenges with unique rewards
- **Cross-Promotions**: Partner with other casual games

### 6.4 Loyalty & Retention Programs
- **VIP/Premium Tier**: Optional subscription for ad-free experience
- **Seasonal Pass**: Battle pass with exclusive cosmetics
- **Milestone Rewards**: Celebrate user achievements

## 9. Creative Strategy

### 9.1 Video Advertising Concepts
1. **Gameplay Showcase**: Simple, satisfying game mechanics
2. **Challenge/Difficulty**: "Can you beat level 50?"
3. **Social Proof**: User testimonials and high scores
4. **Humor**: Funny moments, fails, unexpected twists
5. **ASMR**: Satisfying sounds and visual effects
6. **Speed Comparison**: Speed runners vs. casual players

### 9.2 Visual Identity
- Vibrant, appealing color palette
- Simple, iconic character/mascot
- Consistent branding across all platforms
- Mobile-first design approach

### 9.3 Messaging Pillars
- **"Addictively Simple"** - Easy to learn, hard to master
- **"Challenge Yourself"** - Difficulty progression and achievements
- **"Join the Community"** - Multiplayer/leaderboard features
- **"Play Anytime"** - Quick sessions, casual gaming

---

## 10. Risk Mitigation & Contingencies

### Potential Risks
1. **High CAC**: Ad costs exceed projections
   - *Mitigation*: Tighten targeting, improve creatives, focus on organic

2. **Low Retention**: Players drop off quickly
   - *Mitigation*: Implement retention features, collect early feedback

3. **Negative Reviews**: Technical issues or gameplay problems
   - *Mitigation*: Robust testing, quick bug fix cycles, active community management

4. **Market Saturation**: Similar games already prominent
   - *Mitigation*: Unique value proposition, strong community, regular content updates

5. **Ad Platform Changes**: Tracking limitations, cost increases
   - *Mitigation*: Diversify channels, build organic growth, focus on retention
