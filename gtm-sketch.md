# Go-to-Market Sketch: STACKRIFT
*Launch Strategy & Growth Plan*

---

## GTM Philosophy

STACKRIFT's GTM strategy is built around a **viral-first, data-gated** approach. Instead of spending heavily on paid UA from Day 1, we validate the game's retention and monetization signals in a controlled soft launch, build organic growth infrastructure in parallel, then activate scaled paid UA once LTV/CPI economics are confirmed.

This de-risks the launch by avoiding the #1 mistake in hyper-casual: scaling UA spend before retention is proven.

---

## Phase 0: Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)

### Prototype Validation (Internal)
- Build a playable prototype of the core mechanic by end of Month 2
- Run internal playtesting sessions (5–10 testers, iterative feedback loops)
- Key question: Does the "one more try" reflex trigger reliably?

### Creative Asset Development
- Develop 10–15 gameplay video clips (15s, portrait) for organic seeding
- Capture the most shareable moments: spectacular fails, perfect chain combos, close-call revivals
- Create 3 TikTok/Reels-native creatives with no voiceover — pure gameplay, trending audio overlay
- Build a 30-second app store preview video

### Community Seeding (Pre-Launch)
- Create TikTok and Instagram accounts under @playSTACKRIFT
- Post 2× organic gameplay clips per week starting Month 3 — no paid promotion
- Target casual gaming TikTok communities; test hook formats (challenge clips, ASMR-style block sounds, speed-run attempts)
- Goal: 5,000+ organic followers before soft launch; establish social proof

### App Store Optimization (ASO)
- Keyword research: target terms with high volume and moderate competition in puzzle / physics-casual categories
- A/B test 3 icon variants and 2 screenshot sets in TestFlight/Play Console
- Localize store listing into: English, German, Portuguese (BR), Indonesian, Thai
- Goal: Top-30 organic ranking for "stack puzzle game" category terms

## Phase 1: Soft Launch (Months 5–7)

### Target Markets
Soft launch in **4 markets** chosen for data quality, cost efficiency, and representative behavior:
- **Australia** — iOS-heavy, English-speaking, reflects US behavior at lower CPI
- **Canada** — High-quality iOS data; UA costs 30–40% lower than US
- **Philippines** — Android-heavy, Tier 2 CPI, high casual engagement — tests monetization depth
- **Sweden** — Tech-savvy casual audience; strong signal for European performance

### Soft Launch UA Budget
- Total soft launch budget: **$50,000**
- Allocation: 60% Meta (Facebook/Instagram), 30% Google UAC, 10% TikTok Ads
- Target installs: 50,000–80,000 across 4 markets
- CPI target: $0.60–$0.90 (soft launch geos, Android-weighted)

### Decision Gates — "Scale / Iterate / Kill" Framework

After 30 days of data:

| Metric | Green (Scale) | Yellow (Iterate) | Red (Kill or Pivot) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 Retention | ≥ 40% | 30–39% | < 28% |
| Day 7 Retention | ≥ 18% | 12–17% | < 10% |
| Session Length | ≥ 5 min | 3–5 min | < 3 min |
| IAP Conversion D30 | ≥ 3.5% | 2–3.5% | < 2% |
| LTV:CPI (projected) | ≥ 2.5× | 1.5–2.5× | < 1.5× |

**If Green across all 5 metrics:** Proceed immediately to global launch preparation.
**If Yellow on 1–2 metrics:** Conduct A/B tests on identified weak points; re-evaluate at 60 days.
**If Red on any core metric:** Return to design phase with targeted mechanic/meta adjustments.

## Phase 2: Global Launch (Month 8)

### Launch Markets (Priority Order)
1. **Tier 1:** United States, United Kingdom, Germany, Australia, Canada
2. **Tier 2:** Brazil, Mexico, France, Italy, Japan
3. **Tier 3:** Indonesia, Thailand, Philippines, Vietnam, India

### Global Launch UA Budget (Month 8–12)
- Monthly UA budget: **$80,000–$120,000** (scales with ROAS confirmation)
- Channel mix:

| Channel | Budget % | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Meta Ads (FB/IG) | 40% | Highest volume, best targeting for 18–34 demo |
| Google UAC | 25% | App Store search intent + YouTube pre-roll |
| TikTok Ads | 25% | Lowest CPI, viral amplification potential |
| Apple Search Ads | 10% | High-intent iOS installs; protects organic rank |

### Creative Strategy
- Refresh creative sets every **2 weeks** — creative fatigue is the #1 killer of paid UA efficiency
- 3 creative archetypes, tested simultaneously:
  - **Fail-first hook:** Shows an epic fail, then the perfect run — triggers curiosity
  - **Challenge hook:** "Can you beat this combo?" — drives install intent
  - **Satisfying loop:** ASMR-style perfect run with no commentary — high D1 retention signal
- UGC integration: incentivize top players to post clips; reuse top-performing organic content as paid ads

### App Store Launch Amplification
- Coordinate global launch with App Store feature request submission (Apple Editorial team outreach, 6 weeks pre-launch)
- Launch week coincides with a limited-time starter event: "The First Rift" — exclusive cosmetic for players who reach Zone 2 in launch week

## Phase 3: Growth & Retention (Months 9–18)

### LiveOps Calendar
| Period | Event Type | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Month 9 | Halloween Rift (seasonal skin drop) | D30 retention spike |
| Month 10 | Rift Pass Season 1 launch | Revenue ramp; LTV uplift |
| Month 11 | Winter Zone event | Reactivation of churned users |
| Month 12 | Zone 4 content drop | D90 retention; new content loop |
| Month 14 | Anniversary event + Zone 5–6 | Player milestone celebration |

### Reactivation Campaigns
- Push notification strategy: personalized, event-triggered (not time-of-day batch sends)
- Lapsed user emails at D7, D14, D30 post-churn with "what you missed" format
- Paid retargeting for lapsed users (Meta custom audiences) during season launches

### Influencer & Community
- Partner with 5–10 mid-tier mobile gaming TikTok creators (500K–2M followers) on a revenue-share or flat-fee basis for Season 1 launch
- Launch a Discord server at global launch — target 10,000 members by Month 12
- Monthly "Rift Community Cup" — leaderboard competition streamed on YouTube Live

## Key Risks & Contingencies

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Contingency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soft launch fails retention gate | Medium | High | Return to design; mechanic A/B tests |
| TikTok algorithm changes reduce organic reach | Medium | Medium | Diversify to YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels |
| Rising UA costs compress margins | High | Medium | Invest earlier in ASO + organic; reduce paid dependency |
| Feature request rejected by Apple/Google | Medium | Low | Launch anyway; editorial feature is upside, not dependency |
| Competitor launches similar mechanic | Low | High | Accelerate soft-launch timeline; differentiate on meta depth |
